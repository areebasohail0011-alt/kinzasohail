# Happy Birthday Butt Sahiba — Digital Birthday Gift

A premium, emotional, highly interactive birthday website and digital gift created especially for **Butt Sahiba**.

## ✨ Features & Storyline

1. **💖 Cute Touch & Cursor Heart Sparkle Trail**:
   - Screen par jahan bhi finger tap karengi ya mouse chalayengi, wahan floating glowing pink hearts (`💖`, `❤️`), golden starlight stars (`⭐`, `✨`), aur cute blossoms (`🌸`, `🎀`) softly bloom hokar float karte hain.

2. **Magical Intro**:
   - Displays *"I have something special for someone very special... ❤️"*
   - Interactive *"✨ Start the Journey ✨"* button with sound effects and smooth navigation.

3. **Special Message**:
   - Heartfelt tribute: *"Dear Butt Sahiba, Your friendship makes life brighter, happier, and more beautiful. No matter where life takes us, your kindness and memories will always hold a special place in my heart. ❤️"*

4. **Royal Moroccan Starlight Lanterns**:
   - Ornate hanging Moroccan lanterns with golden domes, arches, and swaying tassels.
   - Tap any dormant lantern to ignite its golden flame.
   - Each lantern reveals its own heartfelt blessing:
     - 🕊️ *سکون و عافیت (Peace & Serenity)*
     - 🌟 *کامیابی و فتح (Success & Growth)*
     - 🌿 *صحت و تندرستی (Health & Vitality)*
     - 💖 *محبت و مخلصی (Love & Friendship)*
     - 🌙 *قبولیتِ دعا (Answered Secret Prayers)*

5. **Birthday Countdown & Big Reveal**:
   - 3... 2... 1... countdown.
   - **🎉 HAPPY BIRTHDAY 🎉 BUTT SAHIBA ❤️** with multi-colored confetti, floating balloons, and fanfare.

6. **Interactive Birthday Cake**:
   - Multi-tier birthday cake with blowable flickering candles.
   - Reveals: *"May every dream in your heart come true. ❤️"*

7. **Ambient Music Player**:
   - Built-in Web Audio API Music Box playing "Happy Birthday" melody (no external broken audio files, no autoplay).
   - Floating toggle button in the top-right corner.

8. **Final Letter & Closing Tribute**:
   - Handwritten letter signed *"With lots of love, Your Friend"*.
   - Closing blessing: *"This little website may close, but my good wishes for you will always remain. ❤️ 🎂 Happy Birthday, Butt Sahiba! 🎂"*

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **React 19** + **TypeScript**
- **Tailwind CSS v4**
- **Framer Motion (`motion/react`)**
- **Canvas Confetti**
- **Web Audio API**
- **Lucide Icons**
